I'm a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Biostatistics and Health Data Science at Indiana University School of Medicine, an Investigator at the Regenstrief Institute's Clem McDonald Center for Biomedical Informatics, and an Adjunct Professor in Purdue University's Weldon School of Biomedical Engineering. I develop machine learning methods for electronic health records, medical images, and other real-world data: to model how diseases progress, to find patient subgroups, and to make sure the resulting models work fairly for everyone.

Before IU, I was a postdoctoral associate with Jiang Bian at the University of Florida. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University in 2022, co-advised by Vincent S. Tseng and Gary G. Yen.
